About Us

Doctor Yuvelirka is a premium jewelry house and restoration workshop located in Incheon, Republic of Korea. We combine curated retail with bench-level expertise so every client receives a seamless experience—from selecting a piece to maintaining it for generations.

Our team works with precious metals, gemstones, and complex settings using proven traditional methods and modern equipment. Whether you visit us for a new acquisition or entrust us with a family heirloom, we treat each project with documented care and transparent communication.

From our address in Yeonsu-gu, we serve local and international customers who value craftsmanship, authenticity, and white-glove service at every stage.
